### Books

#### Software Development
* [Algorithms to Live By: The Computer Science of Human Decisions - Brian Christian & Tom Griffiths](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1627790365/?tag=jwein-20)
* [Code Complete, Second Edition - Steve McConnell](http://cc2e.com/)
* [The Algorithm Design Manual - Steven Skiena](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1848000693/?tag=jwein-20)
* [The Mythical Man-Month: Essays on Software Engineering - Frederick P. Brooks Jr](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0201835959/?tag=jwein-20)
* [The Passionate Programmer - Chad Fowler](http://pragprog.com/book/cfcar2/the-passionate-programmer)

#### Educational
* [The Ten-Day MBA 4th Ed.: A Step-by-Step Guide to Mastering the Skills Taught In America's Top Business Schools - Steven A. Silbiger](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0062199579/?tag=jwein-20)

#### Creative
* [You Can Draw in 30 Days: The Fun, Easy Way to Learn to Draw in One Month or Less - Mark Kistler](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0738212415/?tag=jwein-20)

### Websites
* [Code My UI - CSS Inspiration and Snippets](https://codemyui.com/)
* [Cybrary - Free Cyber Security training.](http://www.cybrary.it)
* [Dev Docs - Combines multiple API documentations in a fast, organized, and searchable interface.](http://devdocs.io)
* [Firecode - Practice Java, C, C++, and Python interview problems.](http://www.firecode.io)
* [Free Code Camp - Learn front end and full stack development.](http://www.freecodecamp.com)
* [Learn You a Haskell for Great Good! - Free guide for learning Haskell and functional programming.](http://learnyouahaskell.com/chapters)
* [Music For Programming - Large amount of background music to listen to while programming.](http://musicforprogramming.net)
* [Radio Garden - Listen to radio from around the world](http://radio.garden/)
* [The Art, Science, and Engineering of Programming - Free online academic journal focusing on programming.](http://programming-journal.org/)
